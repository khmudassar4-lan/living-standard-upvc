What is uPVC?

uPVC is a rigid, durable plastic material widely used for window and door frames because it resists moisture, does not rust and needs little maintenance.

Does uPVC need painting?

No. The colour is part of the finish, so regular cleaning with a soft cloth is usually enough.

Can I choose different colours?

Yes, white and wood-look finishes are popular. Ask us which finishes are currently available.

How do I get a quotation?
